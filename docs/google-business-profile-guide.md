Reviews are the **#1 ranking factor** for the Map Pack. Your website already sends automated review requests when you mark a job as "Won" in the admin dashboard.

### Your Direct Review Link:
```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJM0kDPMxZkWoR4a8_k28XlQk
```

### Review Strategy:
1. After every completed job, mark it as "Won" in your admin dashboard — the system auto-sends a review request email and SMS

## 10. Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't keyword-stuff your business name** — Google will suspend your listing
2. **Don't use a PO Box** — use a real street address
3. **Don't ignore negative reviews** — always respond professionally
4. **Don't use stock photos** — real project photos rank better
5. **Don't set your service area too wide** — focus on suburbs you actually service
6. **Don't forget to update seasonal hours** — incorrect hours frustrate customers
